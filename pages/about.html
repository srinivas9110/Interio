Our Story

We design spaces where life happens beautifully.

Founded in 2009 by a team of passionate designers and architects, Interio has grown from a small studio in Mumbai to one of India's most respected interior design firms — with projects spanning residential homes, boutique hotels, corporate offices, and luxury retail.

We believe that great design is not about trends — it's about understanding how people live, work, and feel. Every space we create is a reflection of its inhabitants: their aspirations, their warmth, their unique story.

With a team of 40+ designers across Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore, we bring together diverse perspectives and deep expertise to craft environments that endure.
